The service workflow

Listen with confidence. Display with intention.

01

Prepare

Connect the service audio, import Bibles you have permission to use, select your default translation and save your settings.

02

Listen & detect

Start the microphone. Local transcription feeds recent Bible-reference detections and candidate Scripture matches.

03

Preview or queue

Click a detection once to inspect it. Queue a bookmark, browse Scripture manually, or prepare the next song or slide.

04

Display

Double-click a detection or use Display when ready. The selected content reaches the projection window and the shown detection leaves the recent list.
