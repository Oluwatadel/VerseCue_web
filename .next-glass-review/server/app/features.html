The desktop workspace

Tools for the whole service.

Listen locally, review Scripture suggestions, prepare the next reading and manage the media your congregation sees.

Built for the booth

One desktop workspace for the service.

From the first spoken reference to the next song, slide or live source: prepare the content, preview your choices, and decide what the congregation sees.

🎙

Local speech recognition

Optional activated AI transcription uses the included verseVIO Clarity model. Optional alternatives download from a dropdown and can be deleted when inactive.

🔎

Scripture suggestions

Follow live transcription, detect spoken Bible references, and find candidate passages from paraphrased Scripture. Review every match before displaying it.

🖥

Preview before projection

Click a recent detection once to preview it. Double-click or use Display to send it to the verse display. The operator stays in control.

📖

Translations that stay in step

Import supported XML or JSON Bible files. Save a default translation change to update manual selections, queued Bible selections and transcription detections.

☑

Queues and service plans

Bookmark detected verses, build a Bible queue, and prepare named service plans. Displayed detections leave the recent list; queued bookmarks remain available.

♫

Songs and lyrics

Prepare songs, select lyric sections, and build a dedicated song queue alongside your Scripture workflow.

▣

Slides and video

Import supported PowerPoint presentations, show slides, and play local video with volume, repeat and playback controls.

◉

Live video sources

Choose an available camera, capture device or screen source. A dashboard shortcut follows the selected source for full-screen video or video behind verses.

✦

Backgrounds and screensavers

Start with two built-in verse backgrounds and three motion screensavers. Add pictures, GIFs or videos, with silent looping for moving backgrounds.

◐

Side-by-side appearance previews

Compare a theme, verse background or screensaver beside Preferences. Preview text and dimming, or watch motion play, before you save.

↔

A workspace you can arrange

Drag dashboard panels into the order your team prefers. Responsive layouts keep settings, songs, slides and service plans usable on smaller windows.

✓

Settings you choose to apply

Changes stay pending until Save in the Preferences footer. Discard restores the active settings. Saved settings apply without restarting the app.

The service workflow

Listen with confidence. Display with intention.

01

Prepare

Connect the service audio, import Bibles you have permission to use, select your default translation and save your settings.

02

Listen & detect

Start the microphone. Local transcription feeds recent Bible-reference detections and candidate Scripture matches.

03

Preview or queue

Click a detection once to inspect it. Queue a bookmark, browse Scripture manually, or prepare the next song or slide.

04

Display

Double-click a detection or use Display when ready. The selected content reaches the projection window and the shown detection leaves the recent list.
